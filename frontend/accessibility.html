Inclusive design

accessibility

zazenware aims to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA across every page. If something does not work for you, please let us know.

Effective: May 26, 2026. Last reviewed: May 26, 2026.

our commitment

zazenware aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, across the site.

Accessibility is a design value, not a checkbox. We want people to be able to browse the art, understand the products, use the cart, submit an order, and contact us without unnecessary barriers.

We treat accessibility issues as bugs. If you encounter something that does not work, please tell us and we will work to fix it.

how we build for accessibility

The site is built with the following accessibility principles in mind:

  • Semantic HTML. Pages use landmarks such as header, nav, main, and footer, with one main h1 per page and a sensible heading order.
  • Keyboard navigation. Interactive controls should be reachable and usable with a keyboard.
  • Visible focus. Links, buttons, form fields, and controls should show a visible focus state when reached by keyboard.
  • Readable contrast. Text should appear on visible blocks or panels, not directly on low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Clear forms. Checkout and contact forms should use labels, helper text, validation messages, and clear error summaries.
  • Meaningful images. Product and artwork images should use useful alternative text when the image communicates important information.
  • Reduced motion awareness. The site avoids unnecessary motion and should respect reduced-motion preferences where motion is used.

shopping accessibility

zazenware uses a simple shopping flow so customers can move from product browsing to cart review without needing an account.

  • Product cards use visible names, prices, images, and action buttons.
  • Shirt products require size and colour selection before the item can be added to cart.
  • The cart badge communicates cart quantity visually and through an accessible label.
  • The checkout form shows field-level errors and a top-of-form error summary.
  • Order confirmation pages display the order number, items, totals, and payment instructions.

known limits

zazenware is a small independent project, and the site will continue improving over time. Some areas may still need refinement as the shop grows.

Current areas we expect to keep reviewing include:

  • image alternative text quality across all artwork and product images;
  • keyboard behaviour for navigation menus on different screen sizes;
  • screen reader clarity for cart updates and validation messages;
  • mobile layout readability;
  • colour contrast across both light and dark themes;
  • embedded third-party content, such as YouTube videos.

Third-party embeds may not be fully controlled by zazenware. Where possible, we provide direct links so people can open the content on the original platform.

testing approach

We plan to test accessibility as part of normal site quality control, not only at launch.

Testing may include:

  • keyboard-only navigation checks;
  • browser zoom and responsive layout checks;
  • light and dark theme contrast checks;
  • form validation checks;
  • screen reader spot checks;
  • automated accessibility scans where useful;
  • manual review of the main shopping and checkout flow.

accessibility feedback

If something on the site is hard to read, hard to navigate, hard to use with a keyboard, difficult with assistive technology, or otherwise inaccessible, please contact us.

Email: contact@zazenware.com

Helpful details include:

  • the page or URL where the issue happened;
  • what you were trying to do;
  • what went wrong;
  • your browser and device, if you know them;
  • the assistive technology used, if any.

We will review accessibility feedback and prioritize fixes based on severity and impact.
