what this is

zazenware is a small artist-run shop and design project. The work starts with visual art, then branches into shirts, patches, paper prints, and music-adjacent design work.

The goal is simple: keep the work direct, independent, human-made, and connected to the underground art and music world it comes from.

ordering art

1. choose items

Add shirts, patches, or prints to cart.

2. submit order

Enter your customer details and Canadian shipping address.

3. send payment

Send an Interac e-transfer to payments@zazenware.com and include your order number.

4. order is matched

Orders begin as pending payment, then move forward once payment is matched.

Canada only for now. Ontario shipping is free. Other provinces and territories use flat-rate shipping.
