how ordering works

The checkout flow is intentionally simple. Orders are submitted through the site, then paid manually by Interac e-transfer.

1. choose items

Add shirts, patches, or prints to cart. Shirts need size and colour first.

2. submit order

Enter customer details and a Canadian shipping address. The backend confirms the final total.

3. send payment

Send an Interac e-transfer to payments@zazenware.com and include the order number.

4. order is matched

Once payment is matched manually, the order can move from pending payment toward production and fulfillment.
